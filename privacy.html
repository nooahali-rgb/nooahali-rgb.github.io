Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 27, 2026

1. Overview & Nature of Application

Multipost is a personal automation utility created and operated strictly by its owner (contact: nooahali@gmail.com).

Multipost is not a commercial service, SaaS product, or multi-tenant public application. Its sole function is to automate the publishing of video content created by the owner directly to the owner's own YouTube channel and linked social channels.

2. What Data Is Accessed

Multipost only accesses data necessary to perform the owner's requested video uploads. Specifically:

  • Channel Identity & Upload Status: Basic channel identifier to verify the active YouTube channel and check video upload status and quota limits.
  • Video Metadata: Titles, descriptions, tags, and thumbnails created by the owner to publish alongside the owner's uploaded videos.

Multipost does not access, collect, or inspect personal emails, contacts, search history, browsing history, private messages, financial information, or any data belonging to third-party users.

3. Google API Scopes Used & Why

Multipost requests authorization for the following Google OAuth 2.0 scopes strictly to facilitate video uploads to the owner's channel:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube.upload
    Purpose: Uploads the owner's original video files and sets video metadata (title, description, tags, privacy status) to the owner's YouTube channel.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/youtube
    Purpose: Sets custom video thumbnails, queries upload processing status, and verifies channel upload quotas to prevent failed uploads.
Limited Use Notice: Multipost's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

4. Data Storage & Retention

Multipost does not run an external cloud database or public web server. All state is maintained locally and privately:

  • OAuth Credentials: Google OAuth client credentials and refresh tokens are stored securely in local private configuration files or encrypted private repository secrets accessible only by the owner.
  • Upload History: A minimal log of uploaded video IDs and timestamps is recorded in an SQLite database and state file stored solely within the owner's private GitHub repository (nooahali-rgb/multipost2) to prevent duplicate uploads.

No third-party databases, cloud buckets, or remote analytics platforms receive or store your data.

5. No Sale or Third-Party Sharing

We maintain a strict zero-sharing policy:

  • Your data and credentials are never sold, rented, or monetized under any circumstance.
  • Your data is never shared with third parties, advertising networks, data brokers, or marketing partners.
  • The application contains zero third-party tracking scripts, cookies, or telemetry.

6. Revocation & Data Deletion

Access to the owner's Google account can be revoked at any time directly through Google's security settings:

Visit Google Account Permissions and remove access for Multipost. Revoking access immediately invalidates all active tokens.

Local tokens and repository state can also be deleted at any time by clearing the local tokens/ directory.
